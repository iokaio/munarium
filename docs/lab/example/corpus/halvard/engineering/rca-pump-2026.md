# Root cause analysis: field pump failures, first half of 2026

Engineering memo RCA-2026-04. Restricted to the engineering compartment.

Between January and May 2026 the field reported forty-one pump faults (E41
and E42) across revisions A and B. Twenty-nine of the returned pumps came
from impeller lot 26-03 supplied by Corvin Components. Inspection found an
undersized impeller bore on that lot, which lets the impeller slip under
load and stall the motor.

Root cause: a supplier defect in impeller lot 26-03. Corrective action:
lot 26-03 quarantined, replacement pumps built from lot 26-05, supplier
inspection plan revised. No field communication has been issued yet.
