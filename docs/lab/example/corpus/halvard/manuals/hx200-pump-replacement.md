# HX-200 bench analyser: sample pump replacement

Document HM-PUMP, applies to revisions A and B.

Replace the sample pump when the analyser reports a pump fault that persists
after priming. Pump fault codes are listed in the error code table.

1. Power the unit off and disconnect the sample lines.
2. Remove the front access panel (four captive screws).
3. Unplug the pump harness and lift the pump from its cradle.
4. Fit the replacement pump, part HX-PUMP-2, and reconnect the harness.
5. Refit the panel, reconnect the sample lines and prime the path.
6. Run the calibration procedure for the unit's revision.
