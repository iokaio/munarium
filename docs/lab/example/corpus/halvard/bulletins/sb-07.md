# Service bulletin SB-07: revision A calibration, manual zero offset withdrawn

Issued 2026-03-02. Applies to HX-200 revision A units running firmware 2.4
or later. Does not apply to revision B.

Step 4 of the revision A calibration procedure (document HM-A-CAL), the
manual zero offset against the 2.0 mL reference standard, is withdrawn.
Firmware 2.4 performs the zero offset during warm-up. Performing the manual
offset on firmware 2.4 introduces a drift of up to two percent at the 2.0 mL
point.

From this bulletin onward, calibrate revision A units by steps 1, 2, 3, 5 and
6 of HM-A-CAL, and apply an acceptance tolerance of plus or minus 0.5 percent
at both points, matching revision B.
