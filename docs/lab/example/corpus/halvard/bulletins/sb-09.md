# Service bulletin SB-09: revision B reagent lot verification

Issued 2026-04-15. Applies to HX-200 revision B units only.

Before calibration, confirm the reagent lot number on the R-1 rinse solution
is 2604 or later. Earlier lots can leave a residue that raises the 1.0 mL
reading.

The acceptance tolerance for revision B remains plus or minus 0.5 percent at
both reference points, as stated in HM-B-CAL.
