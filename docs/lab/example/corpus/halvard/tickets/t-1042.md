# Ticket T-1042: revision A unit reads high at 2.0 mL

Opened 2025-11-14. Unit HXA-00318, revision A, firmware 2.3.

Customer reports the 2.0 mL reading is 1.4 percent high after a pump
replacement.

Technician response: perform step 4 of the revision A calibration procedure
and adjust the zero offset by hand until the reading is within tolerance.
The manual zero offset corrects this every time on revision A units.

Closed 2025-11-16. Customer confirmed the reading returned to tolerance.
