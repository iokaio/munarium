# Ticket T-1077: Meridian Diagnostics reports E22 on two units

Opened 2026-02-03. Customer: Meridian Diagnostics, units HXB-00112 and
HXB-00119, revision B.

Both units show E22 (temperature out of range) at start-up. The customer
asked whether the error affects results already reported.

Technician response: E22 blocks measurement until cleared, so no results
were reported under the fault. Reseated the sample temperature sensor on
both units. Error cleared.

Closed 2026-02-04.
