| R10 | [Record bounded, durable artifact damage](airk-analysis.md#r10) | Implemented, opt-in (`7c6d365`, migrations 0060, 0082, 0083), on the definitions in [artifact-damage.md](artifact-damage.md), amended per ADR 0028 (accepted and amended 20 November 2026). `MUNARIUM_ARTIFACT_DAMAGE_MODE=off|observe|enforce`, default `off`. | ART1 | [Isolate bad copies and confirm fleet blocks](airk-analysis.md#art1) | Implemented locally. A single mismatch isolates only the local copy. Unauthorized, stale, single-match, same-key (in independent mode), revoked, replayed and superseded repairs cannot clear. Verifier key commands: `generate-verifier-key`, `enroll-verifier`, `revoke-verifier`, `list-verifiers`. |
